The buttons that change size are the filter chip buttons located on the "Sales Callbacks" screen, specifically underneath the "TEAM PERFORMANCE" section. These are not the main tab buttons at the top of the screen (like "Callbacks" and "Revenue").

Here is a breakdown of their behavior:

*   **Unselected State:** They appear as dark, pill-shaped buttons with a light outline and white text.
*   **Selected State:** When tapped, the button turns bright blue.
*   **Size Change:** The selected button grows significantly **taller**, changing from a short pill shape to a much taller, almost square shape with rounded corners. They do not appear to grow wider.

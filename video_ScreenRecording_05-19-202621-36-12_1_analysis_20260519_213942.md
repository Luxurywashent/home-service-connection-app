Based on the video provided, here is a detailed breakdown of the two different training module flows shown:

### 1. Legacy Interactive Module Flow (Example: "Engine Bay")

*   **What appears first:** 
    *   Upon selecting the module, the user is first presented with a **"Training Video Required"** screen containing a video player. 
    *   After clicking "I've Watched", the flow moves to a **"Tools & Products"** screen. This screen visually lists the required tools, chemicals, and towels needed for the module before the actual training steps begin.
*   **Step screen appearance:** The step screen (e.g., "Step 1 — Remove Loose Debris") is visually rich. It includes:
    *   A top header showing progress (e.g., "Step 1 of 7") and a star counter.
    *   An image of a vehicle at the top.
    *   The step title and a detailed text description.
*   **Challenge/Quiz appearance:** The assessment is integrated directly into the learning flow. A **"CHALLENGE"** section appears on the same screen, directly below the step instructions. It presents a multiple-choice question with tapable buttons for the answers (e.g., "Remove Loose Debris", "Spray Degreaser", etc.).

### 2. Admin-Created Module Flow (Example: "Engine Bay Dressing")

*   **What appears first:** 
    *   Selecting this module opens an overview screen titled **"What You'll Learn"**. 
    *   This screen simply lists the names of the steps (e.g., "Step 1 Dry Water", "Apply Pink Dressing") and provides two main action buttons: "Start Training" and "Take Quiz". There is no mandatory video or tools list.
*   **Step screen appearance:** The step screens are minimal and text-based. 
    *   They display only the step number, title, and a brief text description. 
    *   There are no images, progress bars, or integrated questions. Navigation is handled by simple "<- Previous" and "Next ->" buttons.
*   **Challenge/Quiz appearance:** The quiz is treated as a completely separate section rather than being integrated into the steps. It is accessed either from the initial overview screen or by clicking "Take Quiz ->" on the final step screen. In this specific example, navigating to the quiz results in a **"No Quiz Yet"** placeholder screen, indicating the admin hasn't added questions.

### UI Issues Noted

*   **Top Banner Overlap:** Throughout the video, there is a red pill-shaped banner at the very top center of the screen. This banner overlaps and interferes with the device's status bar (time, battery) and the app's own top navigation.
*   **Cut-off Headers:** On several screens, particularly the individual step screens for both module types, the top navigation elements (like the back arrow and screen title) are pushed too far up. They appear partially cut off at the top edge of the screen and are obscured by the status bar and the red banner.

### Comparison Summary

The **working legacy modules** provide a structured, multimedia experience. They guide the user through preparatory steps (video, tools) and present visually engaging step screens with immediate, integrated knowledge checks ("Challenges"). 

In contrast, the **broken admin-created modules** offer a much more basic, text-heavy experience. They lack preparatory screens and visual aids within the steps. Furthermore, the assessment portion is decoupled from the learning steps into a separate "Quiz" section, which in this case, is empty and non-functional.

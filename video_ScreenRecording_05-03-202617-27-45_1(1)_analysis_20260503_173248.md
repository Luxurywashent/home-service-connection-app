Based on the video, here is an analysis of what happens to the screen content when a filter button is tapped:

The entire screen content shifts vertically depending on the length of the list that is loaded.

*   **When a filter with a long list is tapped (e.g., "All Positions" at 00:01, "Detailer" at 00:08):** The entire page shifts **up**. You can see the top of the "Team Members" title get cut off, and the row of filter buttons moves higher up on the screen. This happens because the page automatically scrolls down slightly when the long list is rendered.
*   **When a filter with a short list is tapped (e.g., "Admin" at 00:04):** The entire page shifts **down** (back to its original position). The "Team Members" title becomes fully visible again, and the filter buttons move lower. This occurs because the page scroll position resets to the very top when the short list is rendered.

**Conclusion:**
It is not the buttons themselves moving independently, nor is it just the content below them shifting. The **entire page is scrolling**, causing a jarring layout shift. The scroll position is not being maintained when switching between filters that return lists of significantly different lengths.

This is a "Team Members" screen, displaying a list of active employees along with their roles and locations. 

The buttons that change size are the **position filter buttons** located in a horizontal row just below the "Active" and "Archive" tabs. These buttons include categories like "All Positions," "Detailer," "Admin," "Office," and "Ops Manager."

Here is a description of how these buttons look and behave:

*   **Unselected State:** When not clicked, the buttons appear as small, horizontal, pill-shaped outlines with a dark background and white text.
*   **Selected State (Resizing):** When a specific role button (such as "Detailer," "Admin," "Office," or "Ops Manager") is clicked, it dramatically changes shape and size. It expands vertically, transforming from a small horizontal pill into a tall, vertical rectangle with rounded corners. Additionally, its color changes from a dark outline to a solid, bright blue.
*   **Deselection:** When a new filter is selected, the previously enlarged button shrinks back down to its original, small pill-shaped outline. 

*(Note: The "All Positions" button is an exception; when selected, it turns solid blue but remains a small pill shape and does not expand vertically like the others).*

Based on the video, here is a description of the header behavior in the Inbox screen:

*   **Working Fixed Header:** The **Messages** and **Call Log** tabs have a correctly functioning fixed header. When these tabs are selected, the "Inbox" title and the tab navigation remain anchored at the very top of the screen.
*   **Jumping/Moving Header:** The **Emails** tab has a broken, jumping header.
*   **What goes wrong:** When the user clicks on the **Emails** tab, the entire header section (including the word "Inbox" and the three tab links) shifts downwards, creating a noticeable empty gap at the top of the screen. When the user switches back to either the Messages or Call Log tabs, the header jumps back up to its correct, fixed position at the top.
